Interview Rounds

Understand the sequence and expectations of the company selection process.

Round 1 — Aptitude

Quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, verbal ability, and data interpretation.

Round 2 — Technical

Programming fundamentals, OOP, DBMS, OS, networking, and basics of computer science.

Round 3 — DSA/Coding

Problem solving on arrays, strings, trees, graphs, and algorithmic thinking.

Round 4 — Project Discussion

Explain the project, architecture, technologies used, challenges, outcomes, and future scope.

Round 5 — HR

Tell me about yourself, strengths, weaknesses, motivation, company fit, and future goals.
